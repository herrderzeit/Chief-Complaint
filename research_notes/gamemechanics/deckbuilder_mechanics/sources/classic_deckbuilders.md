# Classic "pure" deck-builders (2008–2016+): catalogue of mechanics

Method note: the Dominion Strategy wiki (wiki.dominionstrategy.com) blocked automated fetching with an Anubis bot check, so Dominion detail comes from Wikipedia and search summaries of the wiki and rulebooks. Items marked **[BK]** come from the researcher's background knowledge and were **not verified against a source this session**. They are listed so the catalogue is broad, but the report writer should treat them as unconfirmed or check them (for example the Rio Grande rulebook PDFs linked below). Each mechanic has a generic name, then the game(s) it is attributed to.

## 1. Core loop and genre origin

### Takeaway
Dominion (2008) set the template: everyone starts with an identical weak deck, buys cards from a shared market into their discard pile, and reshuffles the discard pile when the draw pile runs out. Most later games change *what* the market is and *what* the currencies buy. The core loop itself stays the same.

### Cited Findings
- **Identical weak starting deck**: Dominion starts each player with 7 Coppers and 3 Estates, shuffled, with a 5-card hand. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Action / Buy / Clean-up turn with action and buy budgets**: you get 1 Action and 1 Buy per turn; play actions, then play treasures for coins and buy; clean-up discards everything and draws 5. Unused actions, buys and coins are lost. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Gain to discard, reshuffle on empty deck**: bought cards go to the discard pile, which is reshuffled into a new draw pile when the draw pile empties. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game)); [Wikipedia: Deck-building game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deck-building_game)
- **Deck built during play** is what separates the genre from CCGs, where the deck is built beforehand. — [Wikipedia: Deck-building game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deck-building_game)
- Dominion was the first popular deck-builder and spurred Thunderstone, Ascension and Legendary. StarCraft: The Board Game (2007) is sometimes claimed as the first, but that claim is uncited. — [Wikipedia: Deck-building game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deck-building_game)
- **Asymmetric opening hand size to offset first-player advantage**: in Star Realms the first player starts with 3 cards and the second with 5 (3/4/5 with three players). — [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms)
- **Asymmetric starting characters / signature cards**: Puzzle Strike starting bags hold six 1-gems, a Crash Gem and 3 character-specific chips. — [Search summary of Puzzle Strike reviews and rulebook](https://www.boardgamereviewsbyjosh.com/2012/02/puzzle-strike-review.html); [GeekDad](https://geekdad.com/2011/02/puzzle-strike-deck-building-street-fighting-gem-crashing-game/). DC Comics DBG gives each player a starting Super Hero that varies strategy. — [DC review summary](https://www.boardgamequest.com/dc-deck-building-game-review/)
- **Chip/bag building instead of cards**: Puzzle Strike uses chips drawn from a bag, so some reviewers call it "chip-building". — [play-board-games review](https://www.play-board-games.com/?p=2172)
- **Pool-building** is the term for non-card variants of the loop. — [Wikipedia: Deck-building game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deck-building_game)
- **Hybrid deck-builders**: Mage Knight and Arkham Horror LCG graft deck-building onto other systems. Eminent Domain is described as role selection first, with deck-building as a side effect. — [Wikipedia: Deck-building game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deck-building_game); [Strange Assembly: Eminent Domain](https://www.strangeassembly.com/2012/review-eminent-domain)

### Inferences
- The starting deck is a tuning knob for pace: Dominion's 3 Estates are dead cards from turn 1, while Ascension's Apprentice/Militia split and Star Realms' 8 Scout / 2 Viper split pre-commit the two currencies.

### Gaps
- [BK] **Draw 5 at end of turn (not start)** in Dominion, which lets attacks like discard-to-3 hit the next hand. Unverified.

## 2. Market / supply structures

### Takeaway
There are four families. (a) Fixed supply: every pile visible all game (Dominion, Thunderstone village, Puzzle Strike bank, Tanto Cuore town). (b) Random replacing row (Ascension centre row, Star Realms trade row, DC line-up, Shards). (c) Positional or geometric market (Valley of the Kings pyramid, Legendary HQ plus city). (d) Role cards as market (Eminent Domain). Most row games add always-available cards so a bad row never locks a player out.

### Cited Findings
- **Fixed kingdom supply**: shared piles, one card type per pile. Base cards (Treasures, Victory, Curses) are in every game, plus a per-game selection of kingdom piles; an empty pile can't be bought. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Pre-game market selection by players**: Legendary lets players choose which heroes, villains and mastermind make up the game. — [Wikipedia: Deck-building game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deck-building_game)
- **Centre row with replacement (mixed buy/fight row)**: Ascension flips 6 random cards from one deck of heroes, constructs and monsters; a taken card is replaced. — [Ascension rules summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/ascension-deckbuilding-game/); [GeekDad](https://geekdad.com/2010/11/dive-into-the-deck-building-genre-with-ascension)
- **Trade row**: Star Realms shows 5 face-up cards replaced from a hidden trade deck. — [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms)
- **Line-up**: DC Comics DBG buys from a shifting Line-Up of heroes, villains, equipment, super powers and locations. — [Board Game Quest](https://www.boardgamequest.com/dc-deck-building-game-review/)
- **Always-available filler piles**: Ascension has Mystic (rune generator), Heavy Infantry (power generator) and a Cultist monster that can always be fought. — [Ascension rules summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/ascension-deckbuilding-game/). Star Realms has an Explorer pile that is always available. — [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms)
- **Defeated-monster removal (fight row cards don't enter the deck)**: monsters defeated in Ascension are banished from the row and give honour plus an effect; they don't join your deck. — [Ascension rules summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/ascension-deckbuilding-game/)
- **Pyramid / crumbling market**: Valley of the Kings sells from a pyramid. Taking a non-top card makes the cards above drop into the gap, so players can manipulate what becomes available to opponents. — [Board Game Quest](https://www.boardgamequest.com/valley-of-the-kings-premium-edition-review/); [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2014/08/05/mary-dimercurio-prasad-review-of-valley-of-the-kings/)
- **Dungeon hall with depth (rank) penalty**: Thunderstone's monsters sit in ranks of a Dungeon Hall. Deeper ranks need more Light, and each missing Light costs −2 attack (Advance editions use −1 or −2 per rank). Thunderstone Quest replaced this with light as movement depth. — [Search summary of Thunderstone reviews](https://www.boardgamereviewsbyjosh.com/2010/11/thunderstone-review.html); [Strange Assembly: Towers of Ruin](https://www.strangeassembly.com/?p=1490); [Meeple Mountain: Thunderstone Quest](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/thunderstone-quest-review-you-can-delve-if-you-want-to/)
- **Split "village" (buy) vs "dungeon" (fight) turn choice**: in Thunderstone you either visit the Village to buy one card and level heroes, or enter the Dungeon to fight. — [GeekDad Thunderstone](https://geekdad.com/2010/09/review-brave-the-dungeon-to-capture-the-thunderstone)
- **Static mercenary piles plus a contested-resource deck**: Arctic Scavengers has fixed Mercenary piles, plus a junkyard to dig in and a Contested Resources deck fought over. — [Miniature Market review](https://miniaturemarket.com/reviewcorner/artic-scavengers-base-game-with-hq-and-recon-expansion-review); [Islay the Dragon](https://islaythedragon.com/game-reviews/dystopian-digging-in-deck-building-a-review-of-arctic-scavengers/)
- **Bank of chips (fixed supply)**: in Puzzle Strike the bank holds gem chips, puzzle chips and wounds. — [Josh review](https://www.boardgamereviewsbyjosh.com/2012/02/puzzle-strike-review.html)
- **Role cards as the market**: in Eminent Domain, choosing a role takes that role's card into your deck, so you get better at the roles you pick. — [Strange Assembly](https://www.strangeassembly.com/2012/review-eminent-domain); [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2011/10/10/review-of-eminent-domain-tasty-minstrel-games/)
- **Non-supply / special piles**: Cornucopia added a card that puts an extra kingdom pile in play (Young Witch's Bane), plus cards gained only through non-supply methods (Prizes). Dark Ages added more non-supply gains. Menagerie added a Horse pile outside the supply that returns when played. Plunder's Loot is gained only by specific effects. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Mixed / split piles**: Dark Ages piles with several differently named cards (Ruins, Knights). Empires split piles where the bottom card unlocks when the top half runs out. Allies rotating split piles. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))

### Inferences
- "Kicked-up cost" in the brief most likely means DC's Kick pile, a fixed always-available power card that is more expensive than starters; the DC search didn't confirm it.

### Gaps
- [BK] **Kick pile and Super-Villain stack (DC)**: a fixed stack of super-villains, one face-up at a time, bought with power; when one is defeated, the next flips and makes a "First Appearance" attack on everyone. The game ends when the stack runs out. Not confirmed by search.
- [BK] **Explorer scrap**: Star Realms Explorer can be scrapped for 2 combat.
- [BK] **Trade-row scrapping / "clear the row"**: some Star Realms and Ascension cards scrap or banish centre-row cards to refresh the market.
- [BK] **Pile cost tokens and cost reduction**: Dominion's Bridge, Highway and Bridge Troll reduce all costs this turn. Seaside's Embargo puts curse-on-buy tokens on a pile. Adventures uses −1 cost tokens on a pile (Ferry/Plan family). Empires' Debt cards. Cost reduction lets cost-0 cards be gained freely.
- [BK] **Black Market deck**: a private deck of cards not in the supply, with a chance to buy one when played (Dominion promo).
- [BK] **Legendary HQ / City**: 5 HQ slots of heroes bought with Recruit, plus a City row of 5 spaces where villains enter and push along. A villain pushed past the end Escapes.
- [BK] **Shards of Infinity centre row of 6** plus always-available Infinity Shard / Mercenaries.
- [BK] **Hero Realms market row of 5 plus Fire Gems always available**: a Star Realms-family game reskinned for fantasy, with character classes.
- [BK] **Rune Age** (FFG, 2011): scenario-based; each player has a race deck plus neutral, gold and Strength cards; gold is a resource card that is bought.
- [BK] **Resident Evil DBG** (Bandai, 2010): a fixed supply of weapons, ammo and actions, plus a Mansion deck explored face-down. You fight infected using weapon damage, which needs ammo totals.

## 3. Economy: currencies, budgets and tokens

### Takeaway
There are three approaches. Money cards in the deck (Dominion Treasure, Rune Age gold [BK]). Virtual currency from any card (Ascension, Star Realms, DC, Legendary). Two or more currencies, each tied to a target type: buy vs fight (runes/power, trade/combat, recruit/attack, gold/attack + light in Thunderstone). Dominion's expansions then explored nearly every non-card economy: tokens that bank money or actions, debt, a second treasure type (Potion), overpay and alternative costs.

### Cited Findings
- **Money cards (Treasures)**: Dominion's Treasures produce coins in the buy phase. Prosperity adds a higher tier (Platinum, and the Colony victory card). — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Dual currencies, buy vs fight**: Ascension Runes buy cards; Power defeats monsters. — [Ascension rules summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/ascension-deckbuilding-game/). Star Realms Trade buys from the trade row; Combat attacks authority and bases. — [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms). Legendary Recruit (from S.H.I.E.L.D. agents) recruits heroes; Attack fights villains. — [Legendary review summary](https://www.boardgamequest.com/legendary-board-game-review/)
- **Second gating currency (Potion)**: Alchemy adds Potion, a base Treasure needed to buy that set's kingdom cards. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Stored money (Coffers) and stored actions (Villagers)**: Guilds introduced Coffers; Renaissance brought them back and added Villagers. Both are token mats that bank +Coin or +Action for later turns. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game)); [Destructoid expansion ranking](https://www.destructoid.com/dominion-expansions-ranked/)
- **Overpay**: Guilds cards give an immediate bonus if you pay more than the cost. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Debt (buy now, repay later)**: Empires Debt costs give you Debt tokens, which must be paid off before you can buy again. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game)); [Empires rules PDF](https://www.riograndegames.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Dominion-Empires-Rules.pdf)
- **Alternative costs**: introduced in Menagerie. — [Search summary of expansion guides](https://www.wargamer.com/dominion/expansion)
- **Favours (ally currency)**: Allies' Liaison cards give Favour tokens, spent on that game's Ally landscape. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Bonus tokens on piles**: Adventures adds tokens players place on supply piles to boost those cards. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Ante / passive income each turn**: Puzzle Strike turns start by anteing a gem from the bank into your own pile. — [play-board-games](https://www.play-board-games.com/?p=2172)
- **Bonus money for attacking**: in Puzzle Strike each Crash gives +$1 that turn. — [Search summary of Puzzle Strike](https://sirlin.net/articles/designing-puzzle-strike)
- **XP as a separate currency kept outside the deck**: Thunderstone monsters give XP cards that sit beside the deck (not in it) and are spent in the Village to level heroes. — [Thunderstone review summaries](https://www.boardgamereviewsbyjosh.com/2010/11/thunderstone-review.html)
- **Mastery track (permanent progress)**: Shards of Infinity tracks Mastery on a dial; reaching 30 Mastery and playing the Infinity Shard wins the game. — [148apps](https://www.148apps.com/reviews/shards-of-infinity-review); [Islay the Dragon](https://islaythedragon.com/featured/review-shards-of-infinity/)
- **Love (Tanto Cuore currency)**: maids produce Love, which is spent to hire maids. — [The Outerhaven review](https://www.theouterhaven.net/?p=75423)
- **Survey/produce/trade chain**: Eminent Domain players survey for planets, colonise or conquer them, then produce and trade for VP chits. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2011/10/10/review-of-eminent-domain-tasty-minstrel-games/)

### Inferences
- Splitting currency by *target* (buy vs fight) is the post-Dominion standard and maps directly onto a game where some cards treat patients (fight-like) and others buy upgrades.

### Gaps
- [BK] **Multiple buys / +Buy, +Action, +Card, +Coin** vocabulary (Dominion) is the standard notation for a turn budget; confirmed only indirectly above.
- [BK] **Banked trade between turns is not allowed** in Star Realms and Ascension: unused currency is lost, as in Dominion.
- [BK] **Thunderstone Light**: a third resource that offsets the depth penalty (Torch, Soul Gem; also confirmed in the search snippet).
- [BK] **Gold value plus weight/strength requirement**: Thunderstone heroes need strength to equip weapons.
- [BK] **Resident Evil ammo**: weapons need ammo totals in play to be usable, so ammo is a second currency-like resource.

## 4. Deck manipulation

### Takeaway
Thinning (trash/scrap/banish) is the defining skill. Beyond it, games let cards stay out of the shuffle: durations, reserves, mats, private quarters, exile. They also let cards transform, upgrade or "travel" to better versions.

### Cited Findings
- **Trashing (permanent removal)**: trashed cards leave the game. Dark Ages makes trashing and upgrading a theme and adds on-trash effects. Renaissance also has a trashing theme. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Scrap-for-benefit (self-sacrifice)**: Star Realms cards can be scrapped for a one-time benefit; other cards scrap cards without triggering. — [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms)
- **Banish to the Void**: Ascension removes cards from the game; the Empower keyword (Gift of the Elements) lets you banish a card you played that turn. — [Meeple Mountain: Gift of the Elements](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/ascension-gift-of-the-elements/)
- **Duration cards (effect carries to next turn)**: introduced in Seaside and in every Dominion expansion since Adventures. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game)); [Search summary](https://www.wargamer.com/dominion/expansion)
- **Reserve / Tavern mat (park a card, call it later)**: Adventures Reserve cards go onto your Tavern mat when played and are called back later for an effect. — [Search summary of Adventures](https://wiki.dominionstrategy.com/index.php/Adventures)
- **Set-aside mats**: Seaside duration cards are supported by mats and tokens (Island, Native Village, Pirate Ship). — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game)); [DominionStrategy: Mat (search result)](https://wiki.dominionstrategy.com/index.php/Mat)
- **Exile**: Menagerie's Exile mat temporarily removes cards; gaining a copy brings them back. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Return to supply after use**: Menagerie Horses go back to their pile when played. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Upgrade chains (Travellers)**: Adventures Travellers exchange for the next card in a chain until the final level. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Hero levelling with XP**: Thunderstone heroes level up (usually 3 levels) by spending XP in the Village. — [Thunderstone review summaries](https://www.boardgamereviewsbyjosh.com/2010/11/thunderstone-review.html)
- **Starting-card replacement**: Dark Ages Shelters replace starting Estates; Nocturne Heirlooms replace starting Coppers. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Play from the trash**: Nocturne lets some cards be played from the Trash. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Play from deck, not hand (Shadow)**: Rising Sun Shadow cards are played from the deck. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Private quarters (out-of-deck tableau)**: in Tanto Cuore, private maids and buildings go to Private Quarters and leave the deck. — [Tanto Cuore Oktoberfest rules](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0012/3741/8036/files/Tanto-Cuore-Oktoberfest-Public.pdf)
- **Persistent in-play cards (constructs/bases/champions)**: Ascension constructs stay in play until banished and trigger each turn. Star Realms bases stay until destroyed. Shards of Infinity champions work like constructs. — [Ascension summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/ascension-deckbuilding-game/); [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms); [Meeple Mountain: Shards](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/shards-of-infinity/)
- **Hold cards back from the gathering phase**: in Arctic Scavengers, cards not used for gathering are held, hidden, for the skirmish. — [Arctic Scavengers search summary](https://islaythedragon.com/game-reviews/dystopian-digging-in-deck-building-a-review-of-arctic-scavengers/)
- **Discard junk to draw**: base Nightfall lets you discard all wounds in hand and draw 2 per wound. — [Opinionated Gamers: Nightfall](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2011/04/07/review-nightfall-aggression-and-diplomacy-in-deckbuilding/)
- **Chain play by colour link**: in Nightfall each card has a main colour and two link colours. You can chain cards whose main colour matches the previous card's link, and kickers trigger bonus effects. — [Board Game Reviews by Josh: Nightfall](https://www.boardgamereviewsbyjosh.com/2011/02/nightfall-review.html); [Dice Hate Me](https://dicehateme.com/?p=2162)
- **Combine chips into bigger chips**: Puzzle Strike's Combine merges gems into higher-value gems. — [GeekDad](https://geekdad.com/2011/02/puzzle-strike-deck-building-street-fighting-gem-crashing-game/)
- **Entomb (bank cards out of deck for points)**: in Valley of the Kings only cards in your tomb score. You may entomb one card a turn, giving up its buying power. — [Board Game Quest](https://www.boardgamequest.com/valley-of-the-kings-premium-edition-review/)

### Gaps
- [BK] **Cycling / filtering**: Dominion Cellar (discard X, draw X), Warehouse, Library (draw to 7, skip actions). Looters (Dark Ages) discard to draw and gain Ruins.
- [BK] **Topdecking**: Courtyard, Bureaucrat, Scheme, Royal Seal, Herald; also "put on top on buy/gain" via Watchtower or Royal Seal.
- [BK] **Throne-room effects (play a card twice)**: Throne Room, King's Court, Procession.
- [BK] **Remodel (trash and gain a better card)**: Remodel, Upgrade, Mine (treasure to treasure into hand), Develop; the canonical "gain better version" mechanic.
- [BK] **Island**: set aside a card, plus a Victory card, until game end.
- [BK] **Haven / Native Village / Gear**: set aside to get back next turn.
- [BK] **Outpost**: take an extra turn with a smaller hand. Empires, Seaside and Renaissance extra-turn cards (Fleet, Mission, Voyage).
- [BK] **Star Realms Frontiers / Colony Wars**: "acquire to top of deck" effects.
- [BK] **Ascension Fate / Rally / Transform / Unite / Insight / Treasure / Energy shards / Trophy**: mechanics named by expansion; not verified this session.

## 5. Victory and game end

### Takeaway
Victory is either *card points that clog the deck* (Dominion, Tanto Cuore chief maids), *points held outside the deck* (VP tokens, Ascension honour pool, entombed cards, XP-levelled heroes), or *elimination/HP* (Star Realms authority, Puzzle Strike gem pile, Nightfall fewest wounds). Endings are triggered by depleting shared pools.

### Cited Findings
- **Victory cards that clog the deck**: Dominion Victory cards score but do nothing in hand; Curses are negative-VP cards. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Game end on a key pile emptying or N piles emptying**: Dominion ends when Provinces run out or three supply piles are empty. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **VP tokens (points outside the deck)**: Prosperity adds VP tokens. Empires Gathering cards collect VP tokens on a pile until someone takes them. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Landmarks (per-game scoring rules)**: Empires Landmarks change how VP are earned that game. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Shared points pool as a game timer**: Ascension honour comes from buying cards and defeating monsters, and the game ends when the honour pool runs out. — [Ascension summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/ascension-deckbuilding-game/); [GeekDad](https://geekdad.com/2010/11/dive-into-the-deck-building-genre-with-ascension)
- **HP / life total with elimination**: Star Realms authority starts at 50; you lose at 0. — [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms)
- **Gem-pile overflow loss**: in Puzzle Strike, the size of your gem pile decides whether you lose. — [play-board-games](https://www.play-board-games.com/?p=2172)
- **Wound-deck exhaustion; fewest wounds wins**: the Nightfall Wound deck holds 10 per player, and the game ends when it empties. — [Josh: Nightfall](https://www.boardgamereviewsbyjosh.com/2011/02/nightfall-review.html)
- **Contested-resources deck as timer; largest tribe wins**: Arctic Scavengers. — [Search summary of rules](https://gamerules.com/rules/arctic-scavengers/)
- **Co-op boss defeated N times vs scheme loss**: Legendary heroes must beat the Mastermind four times. Players lose if the Scheme completes, for example through villains escaping or wounds. Rescued Bystanders score VP. — [Board Game Quest: Legendary](https://www.boardgamequest.com/legendary-board-game-review/)
- **Alternate instant win**: Shards of Infinity at 30 Mastery plus the Infinity Shard. — [148apps](https://www.148apps.com/reviews/shards-of-infinity-review); Star Realms Missions expansion wins when all hidden missions are done. — [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms)
- **Levelled cards worth VP**: Thunderstone level-3 heroes are worth VP. — [Thunderstone review summary](https://www.boardgamereviewsbyjosh.com/2010/11/thunderstone-review.html)
- **Score only banked cards**: Valley of the Kings tomb. — [Board Game Quest](https://www.boardgamequest.com/valley-of-the-kings-premium-edition-review/)
- **Cards that score but are weak in play**: Tanto Cuore Maid Chiefs mainly give VP. — [Tanto Cuore Oktoberfest rules](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0012/3741/8036/files/Tanto-Cuore-Oktoberfest-Public.pdf)
- **Set collection scoring**: Valley of the Kings rewards completing sets; reviewers note opponents can hand you set completions. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2014/08/05/mary-dimercurio-prasad-review-of-valley-of-the-kings/)
- **Multi-type victory cards**: Intrigue Victory cards also have other types and abilities. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))

### Gaps
- [BK] **Variable-VP cards**: Gardens (1 VP per 10 cards), Duke, Vineyard, Fairgrounds (variety, also confirmed indirectly via Cornucopia's "rewards variety" theme).
- [BK] **Monster-deck exhaustion / Thunderstone card reached**: Thunderstone ends when the Thunderstone card reaches rank 1 or is captured.
- [BK] **DC super-villain stack exhaustion** ends the game; VP printed on bought cards.

## 6. Interaction

### Takeaway
Interaction ranges from Dominion's indirect attacks plus reaction defences, to direct damage (Star Realms, Hero Realms, Shards), to junk injection (curses, wounds, ruins, vulnerabilities, illness), to co-op threat decks (Legendary). Faction synergy (ally/unify) is a self-interaction mechanic that started in Star Realms.

### Cited Findings
- **Attacks**: Dominion Attack cards hurt opponents, for example forcing discards or giving Curses. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Reactions (play off-turn, often as a defence)**: Dominion Reactions trigger outside your turn, often in response to Attacks. Hinterlands 2nd edition has a Reaction subtheme. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Junk / curse injection**: Dominion Curses; Dark Ages Ruins; Nightfall Wounds; Legendary Wounds; Puzzle Strike wounds in the bank. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game)); [Josh: Nightfall](https://www.boardgamereviewsbyjosh.com/2011/02/nightfall-review.html); [Josh: Puzzle Strike](https://www.boardgamereviewsbyjosh.com/2012/02/puzzle-strike-review.html)

- **Faction ally abilities**: Star Realms bonus effects trigger when another card of the same faction is in play. ## 7. Card types, keywords and non-deck purchases (landscapes)

### Takeaway
Dominion's later expansions moved much design into "landscape" cards that you buy or that sit beside the supply without entering anyone's deck (Events, Landmarks, Projects, Ways, Allies, Traits, Prophecies). That is the cleanest precedent for permanent upgrades bought with an in-game currency.

### Cited Findings
- **Base card types**: Treasure, Victory, Curse, Action, Attack, Reaction. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Modal choice cards ("choose one")**: Intrigue. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **On-gain / on-buy triggers**: Hinterlands. **On-trash triggers**: Dark Ages. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Events (one-shot purchases not added to the deck)**: Adventures (first landscape), Empires, Menagerie, Plunder. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Projects (bought once, give a permanent rule bonus)**: Renaissance. Ownership is tracked with player cubes. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game)); [Search summary](https://www.destructoid.com/dominion-expansions-ranked/)
- **Landmarks (shared scoring modifiers)**: Empires. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Ways (alternative resolution for any Action)**: Menagerie Ways change how Action cards can be played. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Allies + Liaisons**: Allies. **Traits (modify a whole pile)**: Plunder. **Prophecies (rule change that switches on later)**: Rising Sun. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Extra turn phase (Night)**: Nocturne adds a Night phase after Buy, with Night cards. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Hidden starting objectives / bonuses**: Star Realms Gambits (bonus cards at start), Missions (hidden objectives). — [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms)
- **Heroes that act on purchase**: Star Realms heroes enter play immediately when bought. — [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms)
- **Events shuffled into the market deck**: Star Realms events trigger when revealed in the trade row. Ascension also has events [partly BK]. — [Wikipedia: Star Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Realms)
- **Variety reward**: Cornucopia cards reward different card names in your deck. — [Wikipedia: Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_(card_game))
- **Kickers (conditional bonus)**: Nightfall. — [Dice Hate Me](https://dicehateme.com/?p=2162)
- **Buildings / Events in Tanto Cuore**: Building cards go to Private Quarters when employed. Event cards can sit in Private Quarters. — [Oktoberfest rules](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0012/3741/8036/files/Tanto-Cuore-Oktoberfest-Public.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Events/Projects split is a direct template for a staff-room upgrade system: Events are one-off purchases, Projects are permanent ward upgrades. Neither bloats the deck.
- The Star Realms ally/scrap pair, and Ascension constructs, are the most transferable "keyword" systems for a game with departments (factions) and persistent equipment (bases/constructs).

# Hybrid Deck-Building Board Games: Mechanics Catalogue

Scope: deck-builders combined with a map, board, worker placement, racing or area control. Each bullet gives a **generic mechanic name**, a 1–3 sentence explanation, the games using it and an inline source. Mechanics that are well known but could not be verified this session are listed under each section's Gaps, labelled "known, not verified", so the report writer can tell sourced from unsourced material.

## 1. How do cards double as actions on the board?

### Takeaway
Hybrids make the card the *ticket* or *fuel* for a board action. A card can carry a placement icon (Dune), a travel cost (Arnak), a location identity (A Few Acres of Snow), a unit identity (Undaunted) or a generic "sideways" value (Mage Knight). Many games split each card into two uses: an early "send a worker" half and a later "reveal for resources" half.

### Cited Findings
- **Split-card agent/reveal (two-mode card):** Each card has an agent section that sends a worker to a board space matching its icon, and a reveal section used on the end-of-round turn. Players alternate agent turns until each takes a single reveal turn. Used by *Dune: Imperium* and *Uprising*. — [Dire Wolf designer diary](https://news.direwolfdigital.com/dune-imperium-designer-diary-1-beginnings/); [Gaming Trend review](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/dune-imperium-review-you-must-not-fear/)
- **Card icons gate worker placement (exclusive spaces):** The icon on the card limits which board spaces you can visit, and only one agent may occupy a space, so blocking and predicting opponents matter. Used by *Dune: Imperium*. — [Gaming Trend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/dune-imperium-review-you-must-not-fear/)
- **Spy/infiltration override:** Uprising's spies let players get past the usual worker-placement blocking, by entering spaces that are already occupied. Reviewers call this the main improvement. Uprising also adds sandworms (a combat amplifier) and contracts. Used by *Dune: Imperium – Uprising*. — [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/games/board-games/dune-imperium-uprising-review); [Ryan Board Games](https://ryan-board-games.pages.dev/reviews/dune-imperium-uprising-review)
- **Travel-cost icons on cards (tiered transport):** Starter cards show travel icons (boot, boat, plane) that are spent to send an archaeologist to a dig site, and a card can be played either for its ability or for its travel icon. Board Game Arena describes a hierarchy: a higher icon can pay for a lower one (a plane pays for a boat), but a plane cost needs a plane. Two coins can also buy a plane. Used by *Lost Ruins of Arnak*. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/lost-ruins-of-arnak/); [Board Game Arena panel](https://en.boardgamearena.com/gamepanel?game=arnak)
- **Location cards (the deck mirrors map control):** Each territory you control has a matching card in your deck, and taking a new location adds its card. Location cards are what you play to settle, fortify and besiege at that place, while "empire" cards supply military strength and special moves. Used by *A Few Acres of Snow*. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2011/09/01/review-a-few-acres-of-snow/); [Armchair General](https://armchairgeneral.com/a-few-acres-of-snow-boardgame-review.htm)
- **Unit cards activate miniatures/counters:** Each soldier card belongs to a specific squad and is played to activate that unit on the tile map (move, attack, scout). Draw frequency therefore equals how often a unit can act. Used by *Undaunted: Normandy* and the other Undaunted games. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/undaunted-normandy-review-deck-building-on-d-day/); [Board-Game.co.uk](https://www.board-game.co.uk/undaunted-normandy-review/)
- **Sideways play (generic value):** Any non-Wound card can be played sideways for a basic Move 1, Influence 1, Attack 1 or Block 1, so no card is ever completely dead. Used by *Mage Knight*. — [Lehi rules summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/mage-knight-board-game/); [Zatu review](https://zatu.com/blogs/reviews/mage-knight-review)
- **Hand-of-roles (action cards, no shuffle):** In Concordia each card in hand is a role, such as moving colonists, building or trading. Cards are played one per turn and are not shuffled. The Tribune card returns all played cards to hand and pays 1 sestertius for each card beyond the third. Used by *Concordia*. — [BGA Concordia help](https://en.doc.boardgamearena.com/Gamehelpconcordia); [Official Game Rules](https://officialgamerules.org/game-rules/concordia-rules/)
- **Spending card power to build network on the map:** Cards lay rails and build stations on a city map (Tokyo/Osaka). The deck is the engine and the map is the scoring layer. Used by *Trains* (Hisashi Hayashi). — [Strange Assembly](https://www.strangeassembly.com/2013/review-trains); [AEG rulebook](https://www.alderac.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/Trains_Rulebook.pdf)
- **Card-limited tactical grid play:** A hex map where units act only through cards from the deck you build, with no dice. Used by *Ascension Tactics*. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/ascension-tactics-miniatures-deckbuilding-game/)

### Inferences
- There are two dominant patterns. The first is the *gate*: the card gives permission to use a space (Dune, Arnak, Undaunted). The second is the *fuel*: the card's points are spent on a generic board resource (Mage Knight, Trains, Heat). Dual-use cards blend the two.
- Location and unit cards make the deck a mirror of the board state, so board expansion directly dilutes or strengthens the deck.

### Gaps
- Known, not verified this session: in *Dune: Imperium*, the reveal half yields Persuasion (buy) and Swords (conflict strength). Troops deployed via agent spaces enter the conflict.
- Known, not verified: *Clank!* cards give Skill (buy), Swords (fight) and Boots (move) on the map. *Star Wars: The Deckbuilding Game* has a Galaxy Row market and a Force track. *Imperium* cards often place region and "Fame" tokens. No source fetched this session.
- *Great Western Trail* (cattle cards played at building stops, hand delivered at Kansas City): no usable source found. The search returned only historical material.

## 2. Deck as a resource for movement, combat, influence and area control

### Takeaway
Hybrids usually split card output into two or three currencies: buying power and board power (Tyrants: Influence vs Power; Clank: Skill vs Boots/Swords). That split creates the central tension: improve the deck, or act on the board. In area-control hybrids, removing cards from the deck to a scoring zone is a further lever.

### Cited Findings
- **Dual currency (buy vs board):** Influence buys market cards. Power deploys troops, assassinates or supplants. Neither carries over between turns. Used by *Tyrants of the Underdark*. — [Shut Up & Sit Down](https://www.shutupandsitdown.com/review-tyrants-of-the-underdark)
- **Adjacency-limited deployment vs anywhere spies:** Troops can only be deployed next to your existing presence, while spies can be placed anywhere and grant presence. Used by *Tyrants of the Underdark*. — [SU&SD](https://www.shutupandsitdown.com/review-tyrants-of-the-underdark)
- **Supplant (replace enemy piece):** A cheaper alternative to killing: an enemy troop is swapped for one of yours where you have presence. Defeated pieces become trophies worth points. Used by *Tyrants of the Underdark*. — [SU&SD](https://www.shutupandsitdown.com/review-tyrants-of-the-underdark)
- **Promote to scoring zone (deck thinning as scoring):** Certain cards move another played card out of the deck into a permanent zone, where it is never played again but scores more VP at game end. The rules call this zone the "Inner Circle". SU&SD's summary used "throne room", so terminology varies between sources. Reviewers call the timing of promotion a constant pressure. Used by *Tyrants of the Underdark*. — [SU&SD](https://www.shutupandsitdown.com/review-tyrants-of-the-underdark); [Tabletop Gaming](https://tabletopgaming.co.uk/reviews/dungeons-dragons-tyrants-of-the-underdark-review)
- **Majority/total-control scoring:** VP come from card values, sites where you hold a majority or total control, and trophies. Used by *Tyrants of the Underdark*. — [SU&SD](https://www.shutupandsitdown.com/review-tyrants-of-the-underdark); [Board Game Quest](https://www.boardgamequest.com/tyrants-of-the-underdark-second-edition-review/)
- **Multi-axis card output (move/influence/attack/block):** Card effects feed movement across the map, influence for recruiting units, and attack or block in combat, so each hand is a puzzle of how to split output. Used by *Mage Knight*. — [Lehi rules summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/mage-knight-board-game/)
- **Shared and personal mana sources:** A shared dice pool (the Source) gives one free mana die per turn and powers stronger card effects. Gold mana is available by day and black by night. Used by *Mage Knight*. — [Lehi rules summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/mage-knight-board-game/)
- **Card values as speed (gear = cards played):** The gear you select sets how many cards you play, and speed is the sum of their values. Used by *Heat: Pedal to the Metal*. — [Opinionated Gamers (Dale Yu)](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2023/01/27/dale-yu-review-of-heat-pedal-to-the-metal/)
- **Pick-one-card movement with deck depletion:** Each round you draw from a rider's deck, choose one card, and move that many spaces. Played cards are removed from the game, which reviewers call "deck preservation" rather than deck-building. Used by *Flamme Rouge*. — [Dized rules](https://rules.dized.com/flamme-rouge/rule/components/energy-and-exhaustion-cards); [Stately Play](https://statelyplay.com/2017/10/30/cardboard-critique-flamme-rouge/)
- **Bolster (adding unit cards into the deck from supply):** Command cards move more copies of a unit's card from supply into the deck, raising that unit's activation frequency at the cost of deck bloat. The squad leader can only bolster its own squad, while the platoon sergeant can bolster any squad. Used by *Undaunted* series. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/undaunted-normandy-review-deck-building-on-d-day/); [iCv2](https://icv2.com/articles/reviews/view/43887/review-undaunted-normandy-game)
- **Casualties remove cards:** In Undaunted, hits force you to remove that unit's cards from your deck, hand or discard. The deck shrinks as the squad is destroyed, modelling degraded command. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/undaunted-normandy-review-deck-building-on-d-day/)
- **Card-count multiplier scoring:** At game end each card's god multiplies a board condition (provinces, colonists, goods and so on), so buying cards sets which board achievements score. Used by *Concordia*. — [Official Game Rules](https://officialgamerules.org/game-rules/concordia-rules/)

### Inferences
- The "two currencies" split is the cheapest way to make a deck interact with a board. Adding a third currency (combat or defence) produces the Mage Knight-style hand puzzle.
- Tyrants' promote is notable because the same action thins the deck *and* banks points, which ties engine quality to the score.

### Gaps
- Known, not verified: *Dune* influence tracks with the four factions (alliance bonus at 4 influence), and *Star Wars: The Deckbuilding Game*'s Force track, which tilts card bonuses. These were not fetched.
- Wording of Undaunted's casualty rule (cards removed from where) should be checked against the rulebook.

## 3. Junk cards as a consequence of board actions

### Takeaway
Nearly every hybrid uses a junk card to make board actions cost deck quality. The common pattern is that a board-level choice (speed, lead position, expansion, scouting, failure) injects a low-value card, and a specific counter-action removes it.

### Cited Findings
- **Heat engine (finite overdrive pool):** Heat cards start in a separate engine stack. Overspeeding corners, shifting two gears or Boosting moves Heat into your discard, where it later clogs hands. If you cannot pay Heat at a corner, you spin out. Cooldown (from 1st and 2nd gear, or Adrenaline) returns Heat from hand to the engine. Used by *Heat: Pedal to the Metal*. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2023/01/27/dale-yu-review-of-heat-pedal-to-the-metal/); [Days of Wonder rulebook PDF](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/staging-daysofwonder/uploads/2024/07/9101-HT-Rules-EN.pdf)
- **Hand-lock junk:** Heat and Stress cards cannot be discarded during the optional discard step, so they stay in hand until removed. The hand refills to 7. Used by *Heat*. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2023/01/27/dale-yu-review-of-heat-pedal-to-the-metal/)
- **Exhaustion for leading or being isolated:** A rider with an open space ahead at the end of the round (no slipstream) gains a value-2 exhaustion card into its deck. When the deck is empty you must play exhaustion cards. This rewards staying in the pack. Used by *Flamme Rouge*. — [Dized rules](https://rules.dized.com/flamme-rouge/rule/components/energy-and-exhaustion-cards); [Stately Play](https://statelyplay.com/2017/10/30/cardboard-critique-flamme-rouge/)
- **Fog of War from exploration:** Scouting new map tiles adds Fog of War cards to your deck. They are usable only to bid for initiative and otherwise waste a draw. A Scout's Recon removes them, and an enemy Scout's Conceal adds one to your discard. This is junk as player interaction. Used by *Undaunted* series. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/undaunted-normandy-review-deck-building-on-d-day/); [Board-Game.co.uk](https://www.board-game.co.uk/undaunted-normandy-review/)
- **Waste from building:** Laying rails, building stations and buying VP cards each add a Waste card to the deck. A card or effect that returns Waste is the counter. Used by *Trains*. — [Strange Assembly](https://www.strangeassembly.com/2013/review-trains); [AEG rulebook](https://www.alderac.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/Trains_Rulebook.pdf)
- **Wounds from combat:** Damage puts Wound cards into the hand. They cannot be played, and resting or healing removes them. Used by *Mage Knight*. — [Lehi rules summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/mage-knight-board-game/)
- **Fear from unsolved guardians/sites:** Guardians you leave unresolved at a site (and some site effects) give Fear cards. Each Fear left in the deck at game end costs 1 VP. The Expedition Leaders expansion adds a Ritual Pile that neutralises them. Used by *Lost Ruins of Arnak*. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/lost-ruins-of-arnak/); [Expedition Leaders rules (RulesPal)](https://rulespal.com/lost-ruins-of-arnak-expedition-leaders/rulebook)
- **Unrest (negative VP and shared collapse clock):** Some actions give Unrest cards, which do nothing, cost VP and are expensive to remove. Taking the last card from the shared Unrest pile triggers Collapse, which ends the game immediately. Used by *Imperium: Classics/Legends*. — [Space-Biff!](https://spacebiff.com/2021/06/15/imperium-classics-legends); [There Will Be Games](https://therewillbe.games/articles-boardgame-reviews/8777-imperium-classics-lets-you-delve-into-the-history-of-the-world)
- **Failure leaves a dead card:** Losing a siege costs the settlement and leaves a now-useless location card in your deck. Overexpansion also bloats the deck with marginal location cards. Used by *A Few Acres of Snow*. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2011/09/01/review-a-few-acres-of-snow/)
- **Spin-out penalty adds Stress:** Failing a corner sends the car back, sets it to 1st gear and adds Stress cards. Used by *Heat*. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2023/01/27/dale-yu-review-of-heat-pedal-to-the-metal/)

### Inferences
- Junk cards split into three kinds. *Tax* junk comes from doing good things (Trains' Waste, Undaunted's Fog). *Punishment* junk comes from failure (Mage Knight's wounds, Arnak's Fear, Heat's spin-out Stress). *Positional* junk comes from where you are on the board (Flamme Rouge's exhaustion). Each kind has an explicit removal channel, which is usually a cheaper or slower play (low gear, Recon, rest).
- Heat's engine is unusual: the junk pool is a finite overdrive budget that the player chooses to spend, not a penalty imposed on them.

### Gaps
- Known, not verified: *Clank!* has no junk cards but adds noise cubes (see section 4). *Dune*'s base game has no junk, though trashing is scarce, which one reviewer noted. — [Gideon's Gaming](https://gideonsgaming.com/dune-imperium-board-game-review/) (trash scarcity only)
- *Great Western Trail* "hand clogging" with low cattle: not sourced.

## 4. Push-your-luck and shared bags

### Takeaway
Clank! is the reference design. Card plays and board moves add your coloured cubes to a public pool, and a dragon attack draws from a shared bag, so risk scales with your own noisiness. Bag-builders (Orléans) apply deck-building to a bag of worker tokens placed on an action board.

### Cited Findings
- **Noise cubes into a shared attack bag:** Some cards and paths make "clank", which puts your coloured cubes into a pool. These go into the dragon bag when a dragon attack is triggered, and each of your cubes drawn deals damage. Reviewers single out the noise-versus-reward risk. Used by *Clank!* family. — [Board Game Quest](https://boardgamequest.com/clank-review); [Casual Game Revolution](https://casualgamerevolution.com/node/1898)
- **Neutral danger cubes that hit everyone:** Catacombs' haunted tiles add a white cube to the bag, which damages every player when drawn. Used by *Clank! Catacombs*. — [Gaming Trend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/clank-catacombs-review-dont-wake-the-ghost-dragon/); [Space-Biff!](https://spacebiff.com/2022/09/28/clank-catacombs)
- **Modular/tile-laid map:** Catacombs builds the dungeon from randomly drawn tiles as it is explored. In! Space! uses a fixed ship shape with reconfigurable module boards, plus hacking and lock mechanics. Used by *Clank! Catacombs*, *Clank! In! Space!*. — [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/clank-catacombs-review); [Nerdologists](https://nerdologists.com/?p=9450)
- **Blind boost flip:** Pay 1 Heat to flip the top deck card and add its value to speed. Stress cards flip until a speed card appears, giving a random 1–4. Used by *Heat*. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2023/01/27/dale-yu-review-of-heat-pedal-to-the-metal/)
- **Bag-building workers onto an action board:** Followers (character tokens) are drawn from a bag and placed on personal action spaces that need specific combinations. Used followers return to the bag, except those spent on Beneficial Deeds. Plague events permanently remove a token. Used by *Orléans*. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/articles/games-we-love-orleans/); [RulesPal Orléans](https://rulespal.com/orleans/rulebook)

### Inferences
- A shared bag lets a solitaire-feeling deck-builder punish everyone at once, while weighting the punishment towards the boldest player. It is a light interaction channel with no "take that" cards.

### Gaps
- Known, not verified: in *Clank!*, damage is tracked on a shared health track, the dragon's rage track increases the number of cubes drawn as artefacts are taken, and the "Dragon Attack" symbol appears on market cards as the market refills. Not fetched this session.
- *Altiplano* (bag-building with a travelling pawn on a location board; Reiner Stockhausen): no source found this session.

## 5. Deck size as a clock, hand-size limits, reset steps and thinning tied to board actions

### Takeaway
Hybrids often turn the deck cycle itself into a timer or a scored resource. Examples are a nation deck that adds a card each reshuffle (Imperium), a non-recycling deck (Flamme Rouge), a recall card that returns everything (Concordia), resting (Mage Knight) and gear-based cooldown (Heat). Thinning is usually tied to a board action or space, not a generic trash card.

### Cited Findings
- **Reshuffle-triggered progression (nation deck):** Each time the draw pile is exhausted, the top card of your civilisation's nation deck is added, which drives the civilisation through its arc. Used by *Imperium: Classics/Legends*. — [Space-Biff!](https://spacebiff.com/2021/06/15/imperium-classics-legends); [Osprey mechanisms blog](https://ospreypublishing.com/ca/osprey-blog/2021/imperium-blog-mechanisms-overview)
- **Shared junk pile as game-end clock:** The game ends immediately when the Unrest pile empties (Collapse). Used by *Imperium*. — [There Will Be Games](https://therewillbe.games/articles-boardgame-reviews/8777-imperium-classics-lets-you-delve-into-the-history-of-the-world)
- **Deck preservation (finite cards):** Played cards leave the game and unplayed cards are recycled, so the deck is a fuel tank to ration across the race. Used by *Flamme Rouge*. — [Dized](https://rules.dized.com/game/NO4Xy2cRQwC3tn7fc_wBCw/FtgCep-xT6yILwYZKxzROg/ive-played-all-my-energy-cards-and-the-face-up-recycle-deck-is-empty); [Stately Play](https://statelyplay.com/2017/10/30/cardboard-critique-flamme-rouge/)
- **Recall card (no shuffle, reset by action):** Cards are played into a discard and only return when the Tribune is played, with a bonus for long chains (1 coin per card past 3). This replaces reshuffling with a timing decision. Used by *Concordia*. — [BGA Concordia help](https://en.doc.boardgamearena.com/Gamehelpconcordia)
- **Market depletion or build-out ends game:** Buying the last personality card or building the 15th house gives the Concordia card (7 VP) and triggers the final round. Used by *Concordia*. — [BGA Concordia help](https://en.doc.boardgamearena.com/Gamehelpconcordia)
- **Piece exhaustion ends game:** The game ends at the end of the round in which a player deploys their last troop. Used by *Tyrants of the Underdark*. — [SU&SD](https://www.shutupandsitdown.com/review-tyrants-of-the-underdark)
- **Rest turn as recovery:** You give up your turn to discard Wounds (a standard rest discards one non-Wound card plus any Wounds, and a slow recovery discards one Wound). Used by *Mage Knight*. — [Lehi rules summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/mage-knight-board-game/)
- **Gear-coupled cooldown and hand refill:** Low gears (1st: 3 cooldown, 2nd: 1 cooldown) trade speed for clearing Heat. Hands refill to 7 each turn. Used by *Heat*. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2023/01/27/dale-yu-review-of-heat-pedal-to-the-metal/)
- **Thinning via unit ability:** A Scout's Recon removes Fog of War, so thinning is a board unit's action. Used by *Undaunted*. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/undaunted-normandy-review-deck-building-on-d-day/)
- **Exile for draw consistency:** Cards can be exiled to concentrate the deck, and exiled cards do not return. Used by *Lost Ruins of Arnak*. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/lost-ruins-of-arnak/)
- **Fixed round count:** Arnak runs 5 rounds, each starting with a fresh 5-card draw. Archaeologists return at round end. — [Meeple Mountain](https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/lost-ruins-of-arnak/)
- **Simultaneous draw at round start:** In Dune, all players draw 5 at once, which differs from the usual draw at end of turn. — [Dire Wolf designer diary](https://news.direwolfdigital.com/dune-imperium-designer-diary-1-beginnings/)

### Inferences
- A fixed round count with a full hand redraw (Arnak, Dune) makes the deck a per-round budget rather than a continuous engine. That suits worker placement and makes thinning less explosive.
- Concordia shows that a "deck-builder" can drop shuffling entirely. Card acquisition plus a recall card still produces deck-building decisions.

### Gaps
- Known, not verified: in *Mage Knight*, Day and Night rounds end when a player's deck runs out (an announced "end of round"), which makes the deck a round clock. *Clank!*'s rage-track acceleration and *Great Western Trail*'s hand-size upgrades (4 to 6) were not confirmed.
- The claim from one search summary that Concordia's first Tribune play "scores all cards" conflicts with the BGA and official rules summaries, which say nothing about it. Treat it as unverified and probably wrong.

## 6. Exhaust/tap, persistent tableau, play area vs deck, and cards that stay on the board

### Takeaway
Persistence is less common than in pure deck-builders. Where it appears, it is a scoring zone (Tyrants' promote), a permanent removal (Flamme Rouge, Arnak's exile), a separate engine stack (Heat) or a supply that sits outside the deck (Undaunted). The search found little sourced material on tap/exhaust tableaus specifically.

### Cited Findings
- **Off-deck scoring zone:** Promoted cards leave the deck permanently and score at game end. Used by *Tyrants*. — [SU&SD](https://www.shutupandsitdown.com/review-tyrants-of-the-underdark)
- **Separate engine/reserve stack:** Heat cards live outside the deck and enter it only when spent, so the engine is a visible persistent area. Used by *Heat*. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2023/01/27/dale-yu-review-of-heat-pedal-to-the-metal/)
- **Personal supply pool outside the deck:** Each player has a supply of unit cards that are not in the deck until bolstered, and casualties can come from it. Used by *Undaunted*. — [iCv2](https://icv2.com/articles/reviews/view/43887/review-undaunted-normandy-game)
- **Pre-game upgrade draft:** An advanced mode drafts upgrade cards into the starting deck before the race. Used by *Heat*. — [Opinionated Gamers](https://opinionatedgamers.com/2023/01/27/dale-yu-review-of-heat-pedal-to-the-metal/)
- **Ritual pile (junk set-aside zone):** An expansion zone where Fear cards stop costing points and fuel rituals. Used by *Arnak: Expedition Leaders*. — [RulesPal](https://rulespal.com/lost-ruins-of-arnak-expedition-leaders/rulebook)
- **Permanent unit removal by plague:** A token is permanently lost from the bag. Used by *Orléans*. — [RulesPal Orléans](https://rulespal.com/orleans/rulebook)
- **Recruited units as a persistent tableau:** In Mage Knight, units recruited with Influence sit beside the player and can be activated (and wounded) independently of the deck. — [Lehi rules summary](https://lehi-innovation.github.io/boardgame-rules/rules/mage-knight-board-game/) (unit recruiting via influence; tableau detail not confirmed)

### Inferences
- For a board-centred deck-builder, a persistent zone such as recruited units, promoted cards or a tableau is the natural place to keep long-term progress, while the deck stays a short-term "action supply".

### Gaps
- Known, not verified: in *Lost Ruins of Arnak*, artefacts and items bought go to the play area or the deck, and the research track gives permanent assistants that exhaust (rotate) and refresh. In *Dune: Imperium*, Uprising's contracts and the intrigue card hand form a separate secondary deck. In *Star Wars: The Deckbuilding Game*, bases sit in play as a health and target layer. In *Clank!*, Companions and devices are one-shot market cards. *Great Western Trail* has persistent building tiles on a shared route, with a cattle deck delivered at the end of each loop. *Trains: Rising Sun* adds no sourced mechanics. *Altiplano* uses bag-building with a location map. *Ark Nova* is a card-driven action-strength rotation, not a deck-builder. None of these were fetched this session, so the report writer should verify them before treating them as sourced.
- No reviews were found that specifically discuss tap/exhaust tableaus in hybrids.
